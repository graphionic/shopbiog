# UX & Design Principles — BiO-G / ShopBiog

This document defines the user experience guidelines, visual design philosophy, and product presentation rules for the BiO-G storefront.

---

## Design Philosophy

The BiO-G storefront must project a **clean, modern, premium, and trustworthy** brand identity that reflects the innovative technology behind BiO-G's odor-resistant, high-performance apparel and socks.

---

## Core UX Priorities

1. **Mobile-First Design:** Over 70% of shoppers browse on mobile devices; layouts, touch targets, and sticky purchase bars must be optimized for mobile screens.
2. **Clean & Minimal Aesthetics:** Avoid visual clutter, distracting popups, and generic template styling.
3. **Fast & Responsive Interactions:** Button taps, attribute swatch switches, and drawer slideouts must respond instantly (`< 200ms` INP).
4. **Product-Focused Visual Hierarchy:** Product imagery, key benefit badges, and clear pricing take visual precedence.
5. **Clear & Non-Aggressive CTAs:** Primary "Add to Cart" and "Checkout" buttons must be prominent without overwhelming the shopper.
6. **Intuitive Navigation:** Easy-to-use header menus, category filters, and predictive search drawers.
7. **Transparent Sizing & Material Info:** Size guides and material compositions must be accessible with one tap directly on product pages.
8. **Readable Typography:** Use curated, readable Google Fonts (e.g. Outfit, Inter) with generous line heights.
9. **Purposeful Micro-Animations:** Motion should guide attention (e.g. smooth cart drawer slide-in, button state feedback) rather than decorate arbitrarily.
10. **Build Shopper Trust:** Display verified customer reviews, security badges, money-back guarantees, and clear shipping terms.

---

## Product Page UX Framework

Every single product page on BiO-G must communicate:

* **What the Product Is:** Clear title, product type, and visual hero image.
* **Why It Is Different:** BiO-G odor-resistant tech, freshness retention, and breathability benefits.
* **Key Benefits & Use Cases:** Daily wear, athletic performance, travel, and comfort.
* **Exact Sizing:** Size chart popup/tab (`HFE Size Guide` ID 3722).
* **Material & Care:** Detailed fabric breakdown and wash instructions.
* **Guarantee & Shipping:** Clear delivery timelines and returns policy (`HFE Delivery & Return` ID 3723).
* **Social Proof & UGC:** Verified customer reviews and rating breakdowns.
* **Frictionless Add-To-Cart:** Responsive attribute swatches, clear quantity selector, and instant AJAX feedback.
